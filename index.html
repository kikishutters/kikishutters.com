About Kiki

I’m a photographer and retoucher based in Ibadan, creating clean, expressive imagery with a focus on portraits, fashion, beauty and editorial work.

My approach is simple — strong images, thoughtful composition and polished retouching without losing what makes the original photograph feel real.
